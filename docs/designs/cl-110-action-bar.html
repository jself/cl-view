CL-110: the ticket action bar, five designs

The ticket dialog draws one row of buttons under the facts line. On a Review ticket with a live session that row holds six buttons, and on a phone it wraps onto two or three lines. Below, first the bar as it is today, then designs A to E. Each one is drawn for the same three tickets, at desktop width (the dialog is 900 wide) and in a 380px phone frame. Menus, popovers and tooltips open when you click or hover them; nothing else does anything. Answer with a letter, or a mix like “C, but with B's menu”.

Button text and tooltips are today's. Where a menu shows a second line under an action, it's the button's current tooltip; the few buttons with no tooltip today got a short one, in italics.
